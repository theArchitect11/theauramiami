# Offer & Pricing Blueprint

## Core Offer
AXIOM / LAB builds intelligent systems, not standard websites.

## Category Pricing

### 1. Service Systems
**Best for:** Realtors, consultants, local service businesses, agencies.

Includes:
- Premium landing page or conversion site
- Lead capture flow
- Intake form
- Automation-ready structure
- Email notification setup

Pricing:
- Entry: $2,500–$3,500
- Core: $3,500–$6,000
- Advanced automation: $6,000–$8,000+

Recommended starting price: **$3,000**

---

### 2. E-Commerce Systems
**Best for:** Product brands, Shopify stores, DTC brands.

Includes:
- Premium product landing page
- Conversion architecture
- Product storytelling
- Email capture
- Optional automation

Pricing:
- Entry: $3,500–$5,000
- Core: $5,000–$8,000
- Advanced: $8,000–$12,000+

Recommended starting price: **$3,500**

---

### 3. SaaS Systems
**Best for:** Startups, dashboards, tools, software platforms.

Includes:
- Product positioning
- Interface design
- Onboarding flow
- Dashboard/UX architecture
- AI-ready intake or support flow

Pricing:
- Entry: $5,000–$7,500
- Core: $7,500–$15,000
- Advanced: $15,000+

Recommended starting price: **$5,000**

---

### 4. Legacy Website AI Upgrade
**Best for:** Old websites that need modernization and lead capture.

Includes:
- Visual redesign
- Conversion flow
- AI-ready form/interface
- Automation-ready backend structure

Pricing:
- Entry: $2,500–$3,500
- Core: $3,500–$6,000
- Advanced: $6,000–$9,000+

Recommended starting price: **$2,500**

---

## Recurring Revenue
Offer monthly support after launch.

Options:
- Maintenance: $250–$500/month
- Automation support: $500–$1,000/month
- Optimization + analytics: $1,000–$2,000/month

## Pricing Rule
Never sell as “website design.” Sell as system deployment.
